# Featured image style for 21DEGREES posts

Based on a review of the featured images on the 144 existing posts (November 2026).

## What the existing images have in common
- Nearly all are AI-generated illustrations. The 1024×1536 / 1536×1024 sizes and `assets_task_…`
  file names match ChatGPT's image tool.
- Most common look: flat or lightly shaded editorial illustration, clean shapes, soft paper-like
  background, muted navy, teal and warm beige, with one warm accent (orange, saffron, mustard).
- Indian people and settings: founders in shirts or suits, shopkeepers, restaurant staff,
  Indian street fronts, the tricolour, Hindi signboards.
- Finance motifs: rupee notes and stacks, rising charts, calculators, ledgers, coins.
- Some images carry a short bold headline in the picture ("WORKING CAPITAL", "VIRTUAL CFO", "GST").
- Recent newsjack posts use busier hand-drawn "sketchnote" doodles. These are the least consistent.

## Problems to avoid
- Dollar signs on money in an Indian finance blog. Always use the rupee sign (₹).
- Style drift between flat vector, 3D cartoon, photo-real and doodle.
- The 21DEGREES logo reused as a featured image (3 posts). Every post gets its own picture.
- Long or misspelled text inside images.

## House style (use for every new image)
- **Medium:** flat editorial illustration with clean vector shapes and soft shading. Subtle paper
  grain. Not photo-real, not 3D, not doodle.
- **Palette:** deep navy (#1F3A5F), muted teal (#3E8E8B), warm beige background (#F3E6D3),
  one saffron accent (#E8A33D), off-white highlights.
- **People:** Indian founders, business owners and professionals; modern, confident, diverse in
  age and gender. Friendly, not goofy.
- **Money:** rupee notes and the ₹ sign only. Never $.
- **Composition:** one clear focal scene that tells the post's idea at a glance; generous empty
  space; centred subject so a square or wide crop still works.
- **Text in image:** none by default. At most a 1–3 word label if the scene needs it, spelled
  exactly as given.
- **No** logos, brand marks, watermarks, real politicians or celebrities' faces, or copyrighted
  characters. For a trending person or event, show a symbolic scene (a cricket ground, a
  mathematician's chalkboard), not a likeness.
- **Format:** landscape 3:2 (exported at 1536×1024). It works for the blog grid and is close to
  the 1.91:1 crop used when posts are shared on LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp.
